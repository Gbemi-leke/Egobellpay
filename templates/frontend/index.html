Built so your money stays yours.

  • You see the name before you send

    The receiving bank confirms the account name first, so money does not go to the wrong person.

  • Your PIN never lives on your phone

    Every transfer needs your PIN, and it is checked on our servers, not stored on the device.

  • Failed transfers come back

    If a transfer does not reach the other bank, it is reversed to your wallet and marked in your activity.

  • A receipt for everything

    Each transaction has a reference you can search, share or send to support.
