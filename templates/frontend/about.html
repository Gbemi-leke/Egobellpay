We build banking that keeps up with people.

EgoBellPay is a digital banking app from Egobell Digital System. We make it quick to open an account, simple to move money and clear what everything costs.

Why we exist.

Too many people still queue in banking halls, fill in forms twice and wait days for a simple transfer. We think everyone deserves banking that works from the phone they already own, at a price they can see before they pay.

What we hold ourselves to.

Safety before features

You see who you are paying before money moves, every transfer needs your PIN, and a failed transfer comes back to your wallet.

Speed that respects your time

Opening an account takes minutes, and transfers to other banks normally arrive within seconds.

Customers first

Every feature starts with a real problem someone had. When something goes wrong, a person helps you fix it.

Open to everyone

We build for traders, students, freelancers and small businesses as much as for large companies.

How your money is protected.

EgoBellPay is not a bank. We work under agreements with banks and institutions licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria, and your money is held in regulated bank accounts.
