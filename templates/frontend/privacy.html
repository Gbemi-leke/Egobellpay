Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it and the choices you have.

Last updated: [DATE]

1. Information We Collect

When you use EgoBellPay, we collect information that helps us provide, improve, and secure our services. This includes:

  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, BVN (Bank Verification Number), and government-issued ID details for KYC compliance.
  • Financial Information: Bank account details, transaction history, wallet balances, and payment card information.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, device type, operating system, app version, pages visited, and features used within the platform.
  • Location Data: Approximate location derived from your IP address for fraud prevention and regulatory compliance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data powers every aspect of the EgoBellPay experience. We use it to:

  • Verify your identity and comply with CBN-mandated KYC and AML regulations.
  • Process transactions — transfers, bill payments, airtime purchases, and card operations.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and other security threats.
  • Personalize your experience — relevant product recommendations, savings tips, and feature suggestions.
  • Communicate with you about your account, transactions, service updates, and promotional offers (with your consent).

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only when necessary:

  • Partner Banks & Financial Institutions: To process payments, verify accounts via NIBSS, and facilitate interbank settlements.
  • Regulatory Bodies: To comply with CBN, NDIC, NFIU, and other Nigerian financial regulatory requirements.
  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties that help us deliver SMS notifications, identity verification, cloud hosting, and customer support — all bound by strict data processing agreements.

4. Data Security

We take security seriously. EgoBellPay employs:

  • End-to-end encryption (TLS 1.3) for all data in transit.
  • AES-256 encryption for sensitive data at rest.
  • PCI DSS-compliant infrastructure for payment card data.
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) and biometric login options.
  • Continuous security monitoring, penetration testing, and audit logging.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as required by Nigerian financial regulations (typically 7 years after account closure for transaction records). You may request data deletion by contacting us — subject to regulatory retention obligations.

6. Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data we hold.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, reach out to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@egobellpay.ng or call 08140000105.
